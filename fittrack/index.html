FitTrack

Fitness appLive

A workout app that helps you plan, track and level up your fitness routine, one set at a time.

Try the live demo All projects
The challenge

What needed solving

Sticking with training is hard when progress is invisible. Notes on a phone don’t tell you what you lifted last time, whether you’re actually getting stronger, or if you hit your goal this week.

Without that feedback, workouts start to feel random and motivation fades.

The solution

How I approached it

FitTrack makes every session count. Plans turn into a ready-to-go workout in one tap, last session’s numbers sit next to every set, and a rest timer keeps you moving.

Progress is always visible: strength trends, personal bests and weekly volume. An XP and levels system rewards consistency, so showing up feels like levelling up.

Key features

What it does

Live workout logging

Log weight and reps for every set, see what you did last time, and get an automatic rest timer that beeps when you’re ready.

Reusable plans

Push, pull, legs or your own. Edit exercises, sets and reps, then start any plan in a tap.

Progress charts

Estimated one-rep max for each exercise over time, personal bests and a bodyweight log.

XP and levels

Earn XP for every set, workout and personal best, and level up from Rookie to Legend.

Weekly goals and streaks

Set a weekly target and keep a streak going, with a ring that fills as the week goes on.

Full history

Every workout saved with its duration, volume and sets, grouped by month.
