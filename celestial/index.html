Celestial

Wedding plannerLive

A wedding planner web app that helps couples organise every detail of their big day, from guest lists and RSVPs to seating charts, budget and checklist.

Try the live demo All projects
The challenge

What needed solving

Planning a wedding means juggling a guest list, RSVPs, meal choices, a seating plan, suppliers and a budget at the same time, usually across spreadsheets, notes apps and group chats.

Details slip through the cracks, numbers stop matching, and the seating plan has to be redrawn every time someone’s RSVP changes.

The solution

How I approached it

Celestial brings everything into one calm place, and every part is connected. When a guest RSVPs, the attending count, meal totals and seating plan all update together, so there’s one version of the truth.

The design matches the occasion: soft ivory, deep plum and gold, with elegant type. It works just as well on a phone at a venue visit as on a laptop at home.

Key features

What it does

Countdown and overview

A live countdown to the big day, with RSVPs, budget and checklist progress at a glance.

Guest list and RSVPs

Track RSVPs, plus-ones, meal choices and notes. Search, filter by side and export the list to CSV.

Drag-and-drop seating

Drag guests onto tables, or tap a guest and then a table on a phone. Tables know their capacity and warn when they’re full.

Budget tracker

Estimated versus paid for every expense, a breakdown by category and a clear warning if you go over budget.

Planning checklist

Tasks grouped from “12+ months” to “wedding week”, with progress for each stage.

Built for every screen

A sidebar on desktop and a thumb-friendly tab bar on phones. Everything is saved in the browser, so there’s no sign-up.
